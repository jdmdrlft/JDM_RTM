JDM - RTM
Outfits editor, fast run, custom K/D, name editor and vehicle editor for GTA V 1.57 on PS4 (GoldHEN).

Made by jdm_drlft

Based on Arise - RTM v2.0.0, by yAmethxst
  Repository:  https://github.com/Amethxst/Arise-RTM-GTAV-PS4
  Release:     https://github.com/Amethxst/Arise-RTM-GTAV-PS4/releases/tag/v2.0.0
  Website:     https://amethxst.dev
  X:           https://x.com/yAmethxst
  Discord:     https://amethxst.dev/discord

Names tab
  By jdm_drlft. It reads and writes the names of the organization, office, MC club, clubhouse, yacht, nightclub,
  acid lab and mansion pets, with a preview of colours, styles, sizes and symbols.
  Code: Names/NamesRtm.cs, under the MIT License (see LICENSE-NamesRTM).
  It uses the name data of the guide at https://amethxst.dev/gta-name (rules and the HUD colour table) and the list
  of image tags "GTA V PC Modded Outfit Name Image Tags" compiled by Max1382 (https://github.com/Max1382/GTA-V-Modded-Outfit-Names-,
  CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/; only the image sources of its examples are used).

Vehicle tab
  By jdm_drlft (Ui/VehiclePanel.cs and the Vehicle folder, under the MIT License). It edits the look of the car you drive and what the garage
  keeps of it. It would not exist without the work and the data of the people below; see "Images and data of the Vehicle tab".

Images and data of the Vehicle tab
----------------------------------

Pictures (chameleon paints, wheels and number plates)
  By XB36Hazard, author of the GTA V Save Editor. They are cropped from screenshots of the galleries of that editor, which is also where the
  names and the groups of the colours (Chrome, Hidden, Matte, Metallic, Metals, Other, Rim, Util, Worn), the names of the wheels and the order of
  the plates come from. They are not made by jdm_drlft. They were cropped, and from the chameleon pictures the badge of the editor and its faint
  watermark text were removed. They are inside JDM - RTM.exe (resource wheels.zip) and are used only as pictures of the options. All rights on
  them belong to their author; if he asks for them to be removed, they will be.

Table of native addresses (Vehicle/NativeAddresses.cs)
  Generated from the file lua/natives.lua of the Velora menu for PS4: native names and their addresses in GTA V 1.57, relative to the
  image base 0x400000. It was checked against the console: the 7 stat handlers of the profile match, and most of its addresses are natives
  registered by the game. It has a few wrong entries (for example GET_VEHICLE_NUMBER_PLATE_TEXT), which the tool does not use.

Garage slot layout
  How the personal vehicles are found in the script globals (the table of global pages, the list of known array starts, the cell of the model
  and the cell of the plate) was learnt by reading the code of the Seren plugin (Serendipity GTAO Mod Menu). No code of it is included. The rest
  of the cells (plate style, colours, wheels, tint, RGB values) were found with the Garage Slot tool of this program.

Colours
  Names, order and RGB values of the 161 base colours: the vehicleColors.json of https://github.com/DurtyFree/gta-v-data-dumps (it keeps its
  own terms). Chameleon paint numbers (161-222): GTA Wiki, "Vehicle Customization in GTA V / Respray Colors".

Horns and plates
  Indexes and names of the first 35 horns: RAGE Multiplayer Wiki, "Vehicle Mods" (https://wiki.rage.mp/wiki/Vehicle_Mods).
